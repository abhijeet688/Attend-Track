01

Overview

AttendTrack is an internal employee attendance and activity monitoring Chrome extension developed and operated exclusively by Recruitment Cell ("we", "us", "our"). It is distributed to employees of Recruitment Cell only and is not publicly available.

This Privacy Policy explains what data the extension collects, how that data is used, where it is stored, and the rights available to employees. By installing and using AttendTrack, employees acknowledge that they have read and understood this policy.

🔒
Internal use only

AttendTrack is a closed, internal tool. Data is never sold, shared with third-party advertisers, or used for any purpose outside of workforce management at Recruitment Cell.

02

Data we collect

AttendTrack collects the following categories of data when an employee is actively logged in and has clicked the Check In button:

Data type What is captured Purpose
Attendance records Check-in time, check-out time, total hours worked, date Payroll, attendance reporting
Online/offline status Whether Chrome is open and the employee is active after check-in Live team monitoring
Idle periods Start time, end time, and duration of idle sessions (no mouse or keyboard activity for 60+ seconds) Productivity insights for managers
Open tab count Number of non-Chrome-internal tabs open at the time of each heartbeat Activity context in monitor view
Tab domains & titles Domain name and page title of up to 10 open tabs (e.g. "docs.google.com — Q2 Report") Activity context in monitor view
Leave requests Leave type, dates, reason, and approval status Leave management
Account credentials Employee ID and bcrypt-hashed password — plaintext password is never stored Authentication
⚡
Monitoring is gated on Check In

No data is collected when an employee opens Chrome, opens a new tab, or logs into AttendTrack. Activity monitoring begins only after the employee explicitly clicks the Check In button and stops immediately after Check Out or Sign Out.

03

How data is used

Data collected by AttendTrack is used solely for the following purposes within Recruitment Cell:

  • Generating daily and monthly attendance reports for HR and management
  • Providing managers with a real-time view of their team's availability and activity
  • Calculating hours worked and identifying late arrivals or early departures
  • Managing employee leave requests and approvals
  • Identifying extended idle periods for team productivity discussions
  • Supporting internal compliance and workforce planning

Data is accessed only by authorised personnel — specifically the employee themselves, their direct manager, and system administrators at Recruitment Cell.

04

Data storage & security

All attendance, activity, and leave data is stored in a Supabase database hosted on secure cloud infrastructure. The following security measures are in place:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using TLS (HTTPS)
  • Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes — plaintext passwords are never stored or transmitted
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) policies are enforced at the database level — employees can only access their own records
  • Database access is restricted to authorised API keys only; no public access is permitted
  • Session data is stored in Chrome's local extension storage, which is sandboxed to the extension and inaccessible to websites or other extensions
🛡️
No screen recording or keystroke logging

AttendTrack does not capture screenshots, record screen activity, log keystrokes, read clipboard content, or access browser history. Only idle/active presence status and open tab metadata are tracked.

05

Data retention

Data is retained as follows:

Data typeRetention period
Attendance records12 months from the date of record
Activity logs (heartbeats, idle events)90 days from the date of record
Leave requests12 months from the date of submission
Employee account dataRetained until the employee's account is deleted by an administrator

Administrators may delete individual employee records or all historical data at any time via the Supabase management console.

06

What we do not collect

AttendTrack explicitly does not collect the following:

  • Screenshots or screen recordings of any kind
  • Keystrokes, typed content, or clipboard data
  • Browser history, bookmarks, or saved passwords
  • Email content, file contents, or document data
  • Microphone, camera, or location data
  • Personal financial information
  • Data from websites visited on personal devices
  • Any data outside of working hours (after Check Out or Sign Out)
07

Employee rights

Employees of Recruitment Cell have the following rights regarding their data collected by AttendTrack:

  • Right to access: Employees can view their own attendance records, check-in/check-out times, and leave history directly within the AttendTrack application
  • Right to correction: Employees may request correction of inaccurate attendance records by contacting their manager or HR
  • Right to deletion: Upon leaving the organisation, employee accounts and associated data will be deleted upon request to HR or an administrator
  • Right to transparency: Employees are entitled to know what data is collected about them, as documented in this policy
  • Right to change password: Employees can update their own login password at any time directly within the application
